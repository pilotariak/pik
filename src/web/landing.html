Pilotariak
Slots API

The service behind Kancha's Créneaux tab, where groups of friends who book a court every week or every other week say who comes.

API contract GitHub
REST /api/v1/
Why pik

Made for a group of friends

A slot is a court booked at a fixed time, every week or every other week. For each session, each player answers whether they come, and the session takes place once enough players answer OK.

🔗

No account

No sign-up, no password, no email. A player joins with an invite link and picks a name, and the device keeps a player token. See kancha ADR-0002.

🔒

Private by design

Only a display name, never an email, a phone number or a location, and only a hash of each token. Only the members of a slot read its data, which is deleted 90 days after the season ends. See pik#5.

🕒

Paris time

Sessions, answer deadlines and session states are computed in Europe/Paris, in winter and summer time. Dates in paths are Paris dates, and instants in bodies are RFC 3339 in UTC.

API

Resources

The routes of the API contract, described by an OpenAPI 3.1 document. Paths are relative to /api/v1. Every write sends a player token, and organizer actions need the organizer role.

📅

slots

Create a slot, list the player's slots, read one with its members and upcoming sessions, change or delete it, rotate its invite code, and close or reopen its list.

POST /slots GET /me GET /slots/:id PATCH /slots/:id DELETE /slots/:id POST /slots/:id/invite-code PUT /slots/:id/list

organizer to change, delete, rotate or close

🎟️

join

Preview a slot from its eight-character invite code, claim a free name, or add yours when it is not in the list.

GET /join/:code POST /join/:code/claim POST /join/:code/members

invite code · claim or add returns a player token

👥

members

Add a name, change a member's role or display name, release a claimed name, or leave the slot.

POST /slots/:id/members PATCH /slots/:id/members/:member DELETE /slots/:id/members/:member/claim DELETE /slots/:id/members/me

organizer, except to leave · the last organizer cannot leave

✅

sessions and answers

List sessions by date range, read one with its answers and state, answer OK, Dispo or KO, cancel a date or change its minimum, places or deadline, and add guests.

GET /slots/:id/sessions GET /slots/:id/sessions/:date PATCH /slots/:id/sessions/:date PUT /slots/:id/sessions/:date/answer PUT /slots/:id/sessions/:date/guests

:date in Europe/Paris · only KO after the deadline

🧩

lineups

Select who plays a session and pair them into games. The session's ETag guards the lineup, so two organizers cannot overwrite each other.

PUT /slots/:id/sessions/:date/lineup

organizer · If-Match: 412 if stale, 428 if missing

🔑

recovery

An organizer creates a recovery link for a member who lost their device. Redeeming it links the member to the new device and keeps their answers.

POST /slots/:id/members/:member/recovery POST /recover/:token

single use · valid for 24 hours
